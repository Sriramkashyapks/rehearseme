# RehearseMe  
  
A smart, AI-powered mock interview platform that simulates real interview environments, analyses candidate performance, and helps users land their dream job.  
  
## The Problem  
  
Job seekers have no realistic way to practice interviews. YouTube videos are passive. Friends are not objective. Existing tools are either too simple (just flashcards) or too expensive (human mock interviewers).  
  
## The Solution  
  
An AI-powered interview platform where a talking AI interviewer conducts a structured mock interview, analyses the candidate's responses in real time, and delivers a detailed performance report with actionable improvement tips.  
  
## Who Is This For  
  
- Tech Professionals & Engineers aiming for high-ROI job switches and senior roles  
- Active Job Seekers preparing for high-stakes, rigorous interviews at top product companies  
- Fresh Graduates transitioning from academic environments to corporate expectations  
- Candidates looking to refine their communication clarity, pacing, and on-camera confidence  
  
## Key Features  
  
- **Resume-Aware Questions**: AI reads your resume and asks personalized questions based on your actual experience  
- **Voice Interaction**: Speak naturally with the AI interviewer using speech recognition  
- **Real-Time Feedback**: Get immediate scoring and suggestions after each answer  
- **Detailed Reports**: Comprehensive analysis of communication, technical accuracy, and confidence  
- **Privacy First**: Your voice and face data are processed in real-time and never stored
